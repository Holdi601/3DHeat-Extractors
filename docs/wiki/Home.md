# 3DHeat Extractors

Free, MIT-licensed tools that get what games know about play out into plain
files: courses and levels as glTF, laps as JSON, matches as Parquet tables - and,
with the **watcher**, everything a player's games publish while they play,
recorded in the background for their own statistics and heatmaps.

- [[What is recorded|What-is-recorded]] - every game, what is recorded and from where
- [[The watcher|Watcher]] - installing, running, where the data goes, statistics
- [[Rules and safety|Rules-and-safety]] - what the tools may read, multiplayer, memory, NVIDIA highlights
- [[Updates|Updates]] - how every tool keeps itself current
