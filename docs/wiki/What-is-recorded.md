## Games that are not recorded, and why

| Game | Why not |
|---|---|
| Valorant | No published local interface. Its local replays (since patch 11.06) need a per-build transform that amounts to recovering a key; the client's lockfile endpoints are not a published API, and the game runs under Vanguard. |
| League of Legends replays (.rofl) | Encrypted, re-obfuscated every patch. (The live game *is* recorded, through Riot's Live Client Data API.) |
| Fortnite | Its replays are encrypted, and Epic's terms forbid reverse engineering them. |
| World of Tanks | Replays encrypted with a key in the game. |
| Gran Turismo 7 | Console only; its telemetry is encrypted with a key recovered from the game. |
| Apex Legends | Its LiveAPI sends full events only in private and custom lobbies; nothing local otherwise. |
| Overwatch 2, Call of Duty, Battlefield 6, Hunt: Showdown 1896 | No local replay, log or game-state output to read. Their statistics come in where Steam keeps them (Steam statistics, Steam Timeline) and as NVIDIA highlights. |
| Any multiplayer game, by memory | Never. See [[Rules and safety|Rules-and-safety]]. |
